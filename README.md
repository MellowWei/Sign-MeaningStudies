## Position vis-à-vis Western Semiotics

**Saussure** (dyadic): signifier ↔ signified, arbitrary relation.
**Peirce** (triadic): sign + object + interpretant.
**Frege**: sense vs reference.
**Derrida**: différance, infinite signifier slippage.

**All of these treat signs as mediators** — between minds and worlds, between signifiers and signifieds, between subjects and objects.

**Sign-Meaning Studies rejects mediation entirely.**

By SE-1, the sign *is* force itself — not between things, but a thing. A force-event in the world. This dissolves:
- The arbitrariness thesis (Saussure)
- The interpretant gap (Peirce)
- The sense-reference dichotomy (Frege)
- The différance regress (Derrida)

**By removing the mediation frame, the problems mediation generated dissolve.**
