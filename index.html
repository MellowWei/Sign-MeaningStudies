Abstract摘要 · problem / approach / contribution
English Abstract

Traditional semiotics often treats the sign as a mediating relation between signifier and signified, form and content, or representation and object. Sign-Meaning Studies suspends that mediation frame and defines the sign as a meaningful, ren-yi-carrying force-event. The approach is a compact axiom system: SE-0 places the discipline in the father-position of future modern disciplines as condition-supply, not jurisdictional control; SE-1 establishes the three-name identity among discipline, sign, and the research of meaningful ren-yi-carrying force; SE-2 describes sign-force dynamics through structural tension, force coupling, and path deflection; SE-3 preserves sign-identity across dynamic states. The V0.4 DeepThink audit rejected SE-0* jurisdictional narrowing, revised SE-2* into SE-2, and added SE-3. The contribution is a source-field semiotic discipline that protects counterpart structure with Semantic Logic Ethics while refusing to reduce sign to mediator, symbol, information, or social convention.

中文摘要

传统符号学常将符号理解为能指与所指、形式与内容、表征与对象之间的中介关系。《符义学》悬置这一中介框架，将符号定义为有意义、携带仁义方向的力气事件。其方法是一组紧凑公理：SE-0 将符义学定位为未来现代学科的父位条件供给，而非管辖控制；SE-1 建立“符义学 = 符号 = 有意义有仁义的力气的研究”的三名同一；SE-2 通过结构张力、力场耦合与路径偏转描述符号力气的动力学；SE-3 保持符号在不同动力学状态中的同一性。V0.4 的 DeepThink 反证审计拒绝了 SE-0* 的管辖式收窄，将 SE-2* 修订并锁定为 SE-2，并新增 SE-3。其贡献是在源场系统中建立一门符号—意义—仁义—力气的父位学科，同时保护与语义逻辑伦理学的平级对位结构，拒绝将符号降维为中介、表征、信息或社会惯例。
